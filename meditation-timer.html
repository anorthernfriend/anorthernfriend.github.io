Human-Factors, Circadian & Acoustic Rationale

Visual Safety · Sub-Hertz Light

WCAG Flash Safety & Slow Breathing Cadence

Light signals stay in slow, breath-paced sub-Hertz frequencies (0.10–1.00 Hz), well below the 3 Hz flash threshold associated with photosensitive discomfort or seizure risk.

Hardware note: Display luminance and closed-eyelid perception vary across OLED/LCD panels and cannot guarantee dark-adaptation preservation.
Circadian & Theme Design

Zion & Patagonia, Starlight OLED & Solar Themes

Soft desaturated palettes (Sandstone Neutral, Warm Sand, Deep Granite, Muted Terracotta, Glacial Teal) reduce glare, Starlight (#000000) turns off OLED pixels in dark rooms, and time-of-day themes follow local solar dawn/dusk angles.

Caveat: Screen color shifts support calmer evening viewing but do not replace light hygiene or medical sleep care.
Acoustic Startle & Envelopes

Exponential Fade-Ins, Fibonacci Steps & Bounded Randomness

Smooth exponential fade-ins avoid digital clicks and sudden startle after stillness, while bounded "give or take" variation (±8% interval / ±5% strength generally; ±0.8% / ±0.5% for gentle wake) keeps bells organic without chaotic unpredictability.

Caveat: Web Audio synthesis approximates physical bronze and room acoustics; native builds target studio-recorded samples.
Non-Analytical Awareness & Privacy

No Streaks, No Metrics, Unadorned Dial & Random Duration

Removing hour totals, streaks, and decorative dial shaders prevents clock-watching and analytical comparison; Random duration ("?" before Begin) and 100% local storage protect quiet attention and privacy.

Caveat: A meditation timer supports practice structure and reflection—it is not a clinical or therapeutic substitute.
